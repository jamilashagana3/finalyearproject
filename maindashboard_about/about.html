About the Department

The Department of Computer Applications at Jayaraj Annapackiam College for Women is committed to empowering rural women through quality and value-based computer education. The department focuses on academic excellence, skill development, innovation, and holistic student growth to prepare learners for professional careers and social responsibility.

Empowerment through Education • Technology with Values • Service to Society

Our Vision

To empower rural women to become agents of social change and liberation, especially the poor and marginalized, by providing quality and value-based education.

Our Mission

To provide holistic development by nurturing intellectual, emotional, physical, social, cultural, and spiritual dimensions of students, enabling them to become competent, self-reliant, employable, and service-oriented individuals with global awareness.
